Delete your Pelada Stats account

This page explains how you can permanently delete your Pelada Stats account and the associated personal data.

You can delete your account directly from the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Pelada Stats app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your account information
  • Your profile information
  • Your profile photo
  • Your authentication credentials
  • Personal information associated with your account

Data that may be retained

Statistics and match history may be retained in an anonymized form to preserve the integrity of historical match records. Any retained information will no longer identify you personally.
